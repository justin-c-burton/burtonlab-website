BURTONLAB@EMORY

Uncovering the physics of complex and non-equilibrium systems

Our research is inspired by observations of natural phenomena, from the molecular to the geophysical scale. Many of these systems exist far from equilibrium, where self-organized patterns and nonlinear dynamics emerge from simple interactions.

Levitated Matter

Particles held in sound waves and plasmas reveal non-reciprocal, many-body dynamics.

Soft Earth Geophysics

Glaciers, ice mélange and granular flows, from the lab bench to Greenland.

Gels and Fluids

Hydrogels, Leidenfrost drops, coalescence, charging and fingering instabilities.
